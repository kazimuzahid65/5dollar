Our story

Because everyone deserves a vacation.

The $5 Vacation Club was founded by Brian and Sherri, high school sweethearts with a shared passion for travel. After raising a family and spending years running a small business, they wanted to make travel information and vacation opportunities more accessible.

Today, the club focuses on an informative monthly newsletter, travel resources and vacation offers designed to help members enjoy an annual getaway without breaking the bank.
